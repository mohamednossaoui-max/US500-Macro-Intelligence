# US500 Macro Intelligence — Public Research Data

This directory contains research datasets generated by the
US500 Macro Intelligence research pipeline.

## Purpose

Research and analytical use only.

This data is NOT:

- a trading signal
- an execution system
- investment advice
- a broker integration
- a deterministic market prediction system

## Core Research Pipeline

1. Economic Intelligence
2. Financial Stress Historical
3. Financial Stress Research
4. Fed Intelligence
5. Event News Intelligence
6. COT Historical
7. COT Positioning
8. VIX Historical
9. VIX Sentiment
10. AAII Historical
11. AAII Sentiment
12. Technical Intelligence
13. Liquidity Historical
14. Liquidity Intelligence
15. Market Breadth
16. Cross Asset Intelligence
17. Earnings Market Reaction

## Data Preservation

The Master Pipeline is non-destructive.

Existing research datasets in `public_data/` are preserved
unless a newly generated dataset has the exact same filename.

This allows downstream research layers, historical studies,
macro context, research context, and validation datasets to
remain available.
