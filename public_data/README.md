# US500 Macro Intelligence — Public Research Data

This directory contains research datasets generated by the
US500 Macro Intelligence Master Pipeline.

## Purpose

Research and analytical use only.

This data is NOT:

- a trading signal
- an execution system
- investment advice
- a broker integration
- a deterministic market prediction system

## Pipeline

The Master Pipeline sequentially executes the research modules:

1. Economic Intelligence
2. Financial Stress Historical
3. Financial Stress Research
4. Fed Intelligence
5. Event News Intelligence
6. COT Historical
7. COT Positioning
8. VIX Historical
9. VIX Sentiment
10. AAII Historical
11. AAII Sentiment
12. Technical Intelligence
13. Liquidity Historical
14. Liquidity Intelligence
15. Market Breadth
16. Cross Asset Intelligence
17. Earnings Market Reaction
